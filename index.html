About

I started out in product marketing at Redgate before moving into design, so I think about how a product is sold as much as how it’s used.

Since then I’ve designed developer tools, screen sharing software and consumer marketplaces. Today I’m Director of Design at ShopMy, a fast-growing creator commerce platform, where I also lead design for Thingtesting.

I do my best work close to research and engineering: shipping small, testing early and learning fast. Away from work I travel as much as I can, and once set out to write about every country in the world.
