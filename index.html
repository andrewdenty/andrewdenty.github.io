Hi, I’m Andrew. I turn complicated problems into simple, useful products.

Work

Approach

  • Simple
  • Honest
  • Tested
  • Shipped

These are the standards I hold my work to. Simple, because products get harder to use as they grow, and someone has to push back. Honest, because good design never oversells or hides what matters. Tested, because I’d rather learn from real people than win an argument about opinions. And shipped, because work only counts once it’s in people’s hands, so I stay close to engineering and release in small steps.

Background

I started out in product marketing at Redgate before moving into design, so I think about how a product is sold as much as how it’s used. Over the last decade I’ve designed developer tools, a design system, software for wireless screen sharing, a public org chart platform and consumer marketplaces, at established software companies and early-stage start-ups. Along the way I’ve led research, grown a design team and learned that the best work comes from designers, product managers and engineers who share the problem.
